# 1. Purpose

This document defines the primary user journeys for the FidNext Technologies website.

The user flows translate the approved:

```text
01-business-discovery/business-brief.md
02-ux/sitemap.md
02-ux/page-architecture.md
02-ux/navigation.md
```

into practical visitor journeys.

The flows are designed to help visitors move from:

```text
Problem Recognition
        ↓
Understanding
        ↓
Evaluation
        ↓
Trust
        ↓
Conversion
```

The primary conversion objective is:

> Book a Free Consultation

---

# 2. User Flow Principles

## 2.1 Start With Visitor Intent

Visitors may enter the website with different questions.

The experience should allow them to quickly find the information relevant to their situation.

---

## 2.2 Do Not Force a Single Path

The website should support multiple legitimate journeys.

A visitor may:

* Explore services first
* Learn about FidNext first
* Review technology expertise
* Investigate how FidNext works
* Review case studies
* Directly contact the company

---

## 2.3 Provide a Clear Next Step

Every major page should provide a logical next action.

Possible actions include:

* Learn more
* Explore services
* View technology
* Read case studies
* Contact FidNext
* Book a Free Consultation

---

## 2.4 Reduce Conversion Friction

The consultation journey should be simple.

The visitor should understand:

* Why they should contact FidNext
* What information is required
* What happens after submission

# 20. Form Error Principles

Form errors should:

* Appear close to the affected field.
* Clearly explain the problem.
* Avoid technical error messages.
* Preserve already entered information.
* Be accessible to keyboard and screen-reader users.
* Allow the visitor to recover without restarting the form.

---

# 21. Form Success Experience

After successful submission, the visitor should see a clear confirmation.

The confirmation should communicate:

1. The request was received.
2. What happens next.
3. How FidNext may follow up.
4. Any expected response timing, if officially defined.

Do not promise a response time unless FidNext has confirmed it.

# 40. UX Decision Rules

### Rule 1 — Support Multiple Entry Points

Visitors may enter from the homepage, service pages, search, social media, referrals, or direct links.

### Rule 2 — Every Major Page Needs a Next Step

Visitors should never reach a dead end.

### Rule 3 — Primary Conversion Remains Consistent

The primary conversion is:

**Book a Free Consultation**

### Rule 4 — Reduce Friction

Consultation forms should remain concise.

### Rule 5 — Match CTA to Intent

Exploration and high-intent visitors should not receive identical interaction patterns everywhere.

### Rule 6 — Do Not Force the Funnel

Visitors should be able to navigate freely.

### Rule 7 — Proof Must Be Verified

User flows must never depend on fabricated testimonials, case studies, metrics, logos, or outcomes.

### Rule 8 — Proposed Capabilities Remain Conditional

AI, modernization, cloud, UX, team augmentation, industries, and other proposed capabilities must only become active flows after validation.

### Rule 9 — Mobile Is First-Class

The same important conversion paths must work on mobile.

### Rule 10 — Accessibility Is Part of the Flow

Keyboard, screen-reader, touch, and error-recovery experiences must be considered from the beginning.
