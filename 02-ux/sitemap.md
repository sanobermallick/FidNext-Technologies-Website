# FidNext Technologies — Website Sitemap

## Agent

**Agent 02 — UX & Information Architecture**

---

# 1. Purpose

This sitemap defines the proposed information architecture and page hierarchy for the FidNext Technologies website.

The sitemap translates the approved Business Discovery findings from Agent 01 into a clear website structure designed to support:

* Business clarity
* Trust building
* Problem recognition
* Service discovery
* Proof and credibility
* Technology expertise
* Lead generation
* Consultation conversion

The sitemap must support the primary website objective:

> Convert relevant website visitors into qualified leads and discovery conversations.

---

# 21. Sitemap Decision Rules

The following rules apply to the website architecture.

### Rule 1 — Do Not Invent

No unsupported service, industry, client, case study, technology, metric, or partnership may be added as a factual claim.

### Rule 2 — Validate Before Publishing

Proposed pages marked with `*` require business validation before becoming final website commitments.

### Rule 3 — Prioritize Conversion

Every major page should provide a logical next step toward consultation.

### Rule 4 — Keep Navigation Simple

The website should prioritize clarity over a large number of pages.

### Rule 5 — Avoid Thin Pages

A page should only exist if FidNext can provide meaningful, useful content for it.

### Rule 6 — Support Business Goals

The sitemap must support the strategic goal of positioning FidNext as a technology partner rather than a generic software vendor.
