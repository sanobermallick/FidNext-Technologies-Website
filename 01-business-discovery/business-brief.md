* Business understanding
* Technology strategy
* Product thinking
* Design

* Technology modernization

# 6. Primary Customer Problems

The new website should demonstrate an understanding of the problems that prospective clients experience.

Key problems include:

## Problem 1 — A Business Idea Without a Clear Technology Strategy

The client may have an idea for a software product but lack the technical expertise needed to define:

* Product architecture
* Technology choices
* Development approach
* Scalability requirements
* Delivery roadmap

---

## Problem 2 — Existing Software Is Limiting Business Growth

The client may have:

* Outdated systems
* Poor application performance
* Difficult-to-maintain software
* Limited scalability
* Disconnected technology systems

---

## Problem 3 — Technology Decisions Carry Too Much Risk

The client may need experienced guidance before investing significant resources into technology.

They may be asking:

* What should we build?
* Which technology should we use?
* How should the system scale?
* Should we modernize or rebuild?
* What is the right delivery approach?

---

## Problem 4 — Manual Processes Reduce Efficiency

The client may depend on manual workflows and disconnected systems that create:

* Delays
* Repetitive work
* Data inconsistencies
* Operational inefficiencies

---

## Problem 5 — Internal Technology Capacity Is Limited

The client may not have enough internal expertise or engineering capacity to deliver a project effectively.

---

## Problem 6 — AI Opportunities Are Unclear

Some businesses may want to explore AI but may not know:

* Where AI can create value
* Which use cases are practical
* How AI can integrate into existing systems
* How to move from experimentation to implementation

# 21. Business Discovery Conclusion

FidNext Technologies has an existing foundation as an innovation-focused software and technology company with software development and IT consulting capabilities.

The proposed website direction represents an opportunity to strengthen the company's market positioning and present FidNext as a strategic technology partner rather than a generic software development provider.

The strongest strategic direction for the website is:

> Understand the business challenge first, demonstrate relevant technology expertise, show credible proof, and guide the visitor toward a consultation.

Before moving into UX and Information Architecture, the proposed service portfolio, target market, client proof, measurable outcomes, and technology capabilities should be reviewed and validated.

The next stage must use this business brief as a primary input and should not invent unsupported business claims.
